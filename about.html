COLLEGE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM · RELEASE 0.1.0

One connected view of college life.

CollegeOS is a multi-college platform concept for bringing academics, administration, finance, communication and student services into a secure, configurable workspace for each institution.

01 · CONFIGURE

Each college, its own workspace

Colleges configure campuses, academic calendars, locale, branding and enabled modules during guided onboarding.

02 · PROTECT

Tenant-scoped access

The production design requires verified memberships, server-side permissions, scoped records and auditable administration.

03 · EVOLVE

Versioned modules

Module specifications and release notes describe the changes, permissions, migrations and support impact for each version.
