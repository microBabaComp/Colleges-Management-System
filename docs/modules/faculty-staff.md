# Faculty & staff

**Status:** planned · **Owner:** College administration · **Spec version:** 0.1.0 · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-07

## Scope

Staff directory, college assignments, teaching roles, workload, leave requests, substitutions, onboarding/offboarding and approval workflows.

## Access and safeguards

Separate ordinary directory information from confidential personnel records. Restrict payroll, absence reasons and performance data. Audit role and assignment changes; revoke sessions and memberships on offboarding. Payroll integrations are optional and jurisdiction-dependent.

## Release notes

Record fields, permissions, integrations, migration/backout, retention and support changes. No staff workflows are implemented yet.
