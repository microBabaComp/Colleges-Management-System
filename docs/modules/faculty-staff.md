# Faculty & staff

**Status:** planned · **Owner:** College administration · **Version:** 0.1.0 · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-07

## Scope

Staff directory, college assignments, teaching roles, workload, leave requests, substitutions, onboarding/offboarding and approvals.

## Safeguards

Separate directory data from confidential personnel files. Scope HR records, absence reasons and exports by least privilege. Audit assignment and approval changes.

## Data and release notes

HR/payroll integrations are optional and jurisdiction-dependent. Record schema, permission, integration and rollback impact. Every release must record changes here and in the project changelog.
