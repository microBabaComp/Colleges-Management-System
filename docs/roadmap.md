# Roadmap

1. **Foundation:** discovery, jurisdiction/privacy review, design system, threat model, tenant schema and CI security gates.
2. **Secure platform:** identity, tenant provisioning, invitations, audit, configuration, backup and observability.
3. **Academic core:** student records, programs, term setup, admissions, enrollment, course roster and attendance.
4. **Operations:** finance integration, faculty/staff, communication, library and campus services.
5. **Insights:** governed analytics, exports, retention automation and college-level configuration.
6. **Production readiness:** accessibility and load reviews, penetration test, restore exercise, incident simulation and staged pilot.

Do not import real student records until security, privacy, operational and legal release gates are satisfied.
